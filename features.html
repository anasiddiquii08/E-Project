Skeleton Tourbillon Automatic Watch Movement

This is an automatic (self-winding) skeleton movement with the plates cut away, so the inner workings are visible.The gears, bridges, and balance wheel are all exposed, and a weighted rotor winds the watch as the wearer moves. The metal parts usually have polished or brushed finishing.

Skeleton Watch Movement

This is a plain skeleton movement, open-worked so you can see the gear train, mainspring barrel, and balance wheel at work. The cut-out bridges leave a lot of empty space, which makes the layers of the mechanism easy to see.
